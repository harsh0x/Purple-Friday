3. ADMIN & PROTECTED WORKSPACE ROUTES (/admin/*)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Protected by Edge Middleware (src/middleware.ts) and verified HMAC session cookie (pf_admin_session).
Unauthenticated visitors are automatically redirected to /admin/login with callbackUrl.

Section: Overview & Intelligence
  - /admin                     Executive Overview & Control Room
                               Dynamic India greeting, live operational funnel strip, 7/30/90-day Net Revenue & Net Margin charts, category distribution donut, animated count-up KPI tiles, needs attention alerts, and recent activity feed.
  - /admin/analytics           Operational Analytics
                               Throughput funnel, Average Order Value (AOV), repeat corporate buyer retention rate, sample-to-bulk conversion, on-time dispatch rate, and seasonal spike analysis.
  - /admin/margin              True Net Margin Intelligence
                               Unit-economic profitability analysis per order accounting for blanks COGS, print consumables, packaging, payment gateway fees (2.36%), freight, and RTO return provisions.
  - /admin/customers           Corporate CRM & RFM Segments
                               Recency, Frequency, Monetary (RFM) segmentation (Champions, Loyal, At-Risk, Needs Attention, Lost), corporate client profiles, GSTIN validation, and lifetime purchase history.

Section: Operations & Production Pipeline
  - /admin/orders              Orders Master Control
                               Searchable order table with status filter tabs (All, Pending Approval, In Production, Dispatched, Delivered, RTO), risk score flags, partial-COD badges, and CSV export.
  - /admin/orders/[orderNumber] Order Control Room & Action Hub
                               12-state transition hub with optimistic locking, customer contact card, artwork download, proof status, jobsheet compiler, split dispatch trigger, balance collection, and immutable audit timeline.
  - /admin/designs             Designer Workstation
                               Prepress queue of orders awaiting vector artwork preparation, 300 DPI inspection, mockup rendering, and customer proof dispatch.
  - /admin/production          Factory Floor Station
                               Live print stations grouped by technology (DTF, Screen, Embroidery, UV Sublimation), printable A4/A5 PDF jobsheets, QC pass/fail triggers, and rework logging on IN_PRINTING.
  - /admin/quotes              B2B RFQ & Quote Studio
                               Corporate proforma quote builder, custom quantity slab pricing, printable GST PDF quotes, and dynamic Razorpay payment link generation.
  - /admin/dispatch            Packaging & Dispatch Hub
                               Packaging batches, courier assignment, single/multi-destination dispatch, split shipping CSV upload, and courier thermal label generation.
  - /admin/shipments           Logistics & Courier Tracking
                               Real-time Shiprocket AWB status synchronization, delivery SLA telemetry, NDR exceptions, and RTO return monitoring.
  - /admin/payments            Payments & Reconciliations
                               Partial COD advance tracking, balance-on-delivery collections, Razorpay capture logs, and refund tracking.
  - /admin/support             Customer Support Helpdesk
                               Customer inquiry tickets, order issue resolution, return requests, and cancellation reviews.

Section: Catalogue & Inventory
  - /admin/products            Product Catalogue Manager
                               Active/draft product catalog list, best-seller toggles, inventory availability, and quantity slab summaries.
  - /admin/products/[slug]     Product Atelier Studio
                               Deep product configurator: categories, audiences, MOQ, quantity steps, GST rate, blank item COGS, print cost per piece, colour swatches, and slab discount tiers.
  - /admin/inventory           Raw Blanks Inventory
                               Blank stock tracking by colour and size SKU combinations, low-stock safety thresholds, reorder warnings, and inventory intake logs.
  - /admin/forecasting         Demand & Stockout Forecast
                               14-day predictive replenishment forecast based on recent sales velocity, reorder point calculations, and supplier lead-time buffers.
  - /admin/kits                Corporate Bundles & Gift Kits
                               Multi-item corporate swag kit builder with dynamic bundle discount calculations and gift box packaging selection.
  - /admin/coupons             Discounts & Promotions
                               Coupon code manager supporting percentage and flat discounts, FIRSTORDER restrictions, minimum subtotal gates, maximum discount caps, and atomic usage counters.
  - /admin/reviews             Verified Review Moderation
                               Post-delivery review moderation queue with craftsmanship breakdown ratings (print clarity, fabric feel, color match) and approve/reject controls.

Section: System Administration & Governance
  - /admin/team                Staff Management & RBAC (Super Admin Only)
                               Staff user invitations, role assignment (6 RBAC roles), session revocations, password resets, and account unlock controls.
  - /admin/audit               Immutable Audit Trail (Super Admin & Finance)
                               Append-only PostgreSQL audit log viewer with actor, entity, date range filters, and CSV export secured against formula injection (safeCsvCell).
  - /admin/settings            Global System Configuration (Super Admin Only)
                               Business profile, GSTIN, default shipping rates, partial COD advance percentage (20–30%), max COD value, RTO risk thresholds, and courier credentials.
  - /admin/cms                 No-Code Storefront CMS (Super Admin Only)
                               Homepage hero copy, scheduled announcement banners, section ordering & visibility, FAQs, footer links, SEO meta tags, live preview tokens, and 10-snapshot version rollback.
  - /admin/notifications       Staff Alert Centre
                               Internal notification center for urgent proof approvals, low-stock warnings, overdue production jobs (>48h), and cancellation requests.

4. KEY ADMIN API ENDPOINTS (/api/admin/*)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
All admin API routes require valid staff session authentication, Origin checks, Zod validation, and rate limiting:

  - POST /api/admin/auth/login              Staff authentication and session token issuance.
  - POST /api/admin/auth/logout             Staff session revocation and cookie clearing.
  - GET  /api/admin/auth/session            Current authenticated staff profile and permissions.
  - POST /api/admin/auth/change-password    Forced credential rotation.
  - GET  /api/admin/orders                  Filterable, paginated orders query.
  - GET  /api/admin/orders/export           Sanitized CSV order export.
  - GET  /api/admin/orders/[orderNumber]    Detailed single order lookup.
  - POST /api/admin/orders/[orderNumber]/status      Optimistic version-checked state transition.
  - POST /api/admin/orders/[orderNumber]/transition  State transition with audit note.
  - POST /api/admin/orders/[orderNumber]/override-risk Super Admin manual COD risk override.
  - POST /api/admin/orders/[orderNumber]/collect-balance Marks COD balance collected upon delivery.
  - POST /api/admin/orders/[orderNumber]/refund     Full/partial refund execution via Razorpay.
  - POST /api/admin/orders/[orderNumber]/jobsheet   PDF factory jobsheet compiler.
  - POST /api/admin/orders/[orderNumber]/rework     QC defect logger returning item to IN_PRINTING.
  - POST /api/admin/orders/[orderNumber]/send-mockup Customer mockup review notification dispatch.
  - POST /api/admin/artwork/ingest          25MB magic-byte validated vector logo upload.
  - GET  /api/admin/dispatch/template       Standard CSV template for split-address dispatches.
  - POST /api/admin/orders/[orderNumber]/split-preview Validates split shipping addresses.
  - POST /api/admin/orders/[orderNumber]/split-dispatch Executes multi-destination courier booking.
  - GET  /api/admin/products                Catalogue product listing and creation.
  - GET  /api/admin/inventory               Blanks inventory balances and stock intake.
  - GET  /api/admin/forecasting             Predictive stock-out dates and reorder volumes.
  - GET  /api/admin/quotes                  Corporate RFQs and proforma invoices.
  - GET  /api/admin/quotes/[id]/pdf         Official GST-compliant PDF quote generator.
  - POST /api/admin/quotes/[id]/payment-link Razorpay payment link dispatcher for quotes.
  - GET  /api/admin/overview                Real-time metrics for admin command centre.
  - GET  /api/admin/analytics               Operational throughput, AOV, and retention KPIs.
  - GET  /api/admin/margin                  True Net Margin calculation per order.
  - GET  /api/admin/customers               Customer database with RFM segmentation.
  - GET  /api/admin/cms                     Storefront CMS document (draft & published).
  - POST /api/admin/cms                     Saves drafts, publishes content, or restores versions.
  - GET  /api/admin/team                    Staff member list and invitation handler.
  - GET  /api/admin/audit                   Append-only audit trail search and CSV export.
  - GET  /api/admin/settings                System configuration viewer and updater.
